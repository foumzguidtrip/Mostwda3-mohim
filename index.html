Foum Zguid Trip began at a family fire beneath the palms of Foum Zguid, where the Anti-Atlas bows to the great erg. We are not an agency that discovered the desert — we are the desert, opening its own door.

Our guides read the dunes like scripture: every crest a landmark, every wind a rumour. We carry you over the golden sea of Erg Chigaga, across the fossil mirror of Lake Iriki, to camps where the only soundtrack is drums, wind and an impossible sky.

No buses. No crowds. No hurry. Six guests at most, a private 4×4, and hospitality as old as the caravan roads themselves.

— Hassan, son of the oasis
